**Contents:**
- 702 US occupations
- Automation probability scores (0.0–1.0) per occupation
- SOC (Standard Occupational Classification) group labels
- Total employment figures per occupation

**Original automation probability methodology:**
The automation probability scores in this dataset are derived from or inspired by the landmark study:

> Frey, C. B., & Osborne, M. A. (2017). The future of employment: How susceptible are jobs to computerisation? *Technological Forecasting and Social Change*, 114, 254–280.

This study estimated the probability of computerization for 702 US occupations using O*NET task and skill data. The Kaggle dataset packages these estimates with employment figures.

**Important limitation:** The automation probability scores are pre-computed estimates from the source dataset, not generated by a model trained in this project.

## Contextual Claims

The application previously included rotating "Did You Know?" facts attributed to McKinsey and the World Economic Forum. These have been removed or updated because:

- The McKinsey Global Institute projection ("up to 30% of jobs automated by 2030") dates from a 2017 report and should be read with that context.
- The WEF "97 million new jobs" figure was a projection for 2025 and is now dated.

Any claims citing external research should include author, title, publication year, and URL.
